The project

Context

Hilborn & Howell partners with national banking institutions. In my role I process dozens of $100K+ loan applications every week and present risk assessments to more than 30 banking partners across 10+ states. At that volume, every evaluation needs to be consistent and correct, not just fast.

What I built

I designed and tested an integrated AI-powered system to support accuracy and reliability across our loan evaluation process.

Working within confidentiality

Our covenants with banking partners require strict client confidentiality and data privacy across every document. The system was built to uphold those standards.

Because of those agreements, this page describes the approach at a high level. It doesn't show client data or internal tools.
